# Code of conduct

Kestrix follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarizes it. The linked text is the one that applies.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and discussions, and when you represent the project elsewhere. Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Ruthvik Bandari, at bandari.ru@northeastern.edu. Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or warn, suspend or ban participants, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban and permanent ban.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
